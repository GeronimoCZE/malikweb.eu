# malikweb.eu

The personal website of Nikolas Malík, available in English and Czech.

It introduces Nikolas, his skills and the services he offers, shows a portfolio of his projects, and has a contact form so visitors can get in touch. The site has a light and a dark look, smooth animations, and works well on phones.

Nikolas manages his projects and reads messages from the contact form on a private admin page, without touching the website itself. After signing in he stays signed in for a week, and an admin bar across the top of every page shows new messages and lets him jump straight to editing what he is looking at.
